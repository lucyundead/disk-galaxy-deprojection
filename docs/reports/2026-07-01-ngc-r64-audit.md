# NGC 4321 + NGC 4371 figure audit with the shipped R=64 bundle (+ NGC 4371 vs MGE)

**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Goal:** regenerate the NGC 4321 and NGC 4371 deprojections with the **shipped R=64
bundle** (`dgdp.deproject`, `src/dgdp/models/dgdp_fixed_dict.npz`) — the earlier validation
used the R=32 model — and redo the full one-to-one NGC 4371 vs MGE comparison.
**Branch:** `codex/milestone2-tng-ingestion-design` (not committed).
**Bundle grid:** n_r=64, n_phi=48, n_z=32, z_max=5 kpc, log-R 0.05→30 kpc.

## TL;DR

**R=64 reproduces the R=32 validation and improves the outer-disk vertical match.** The
learned vertical structure still matches Behzad Tahmasebzadeh's independent MGE to ~15–20 %
(RMS|z| learned/MGE 0.97–1.20 over R=0.5–12 kpc, vs 0.80–1.21 at R=32 — the R=12 point went
0.80→0.98). The bar amplitude is unchanged (A2 peak 0.33 @ 3.4 kpc vs MGE 0.17 @ 2.3 kpc).
The one real change: the finer radial grid resolves a **slightly more concentrated inner
disk** (R½ 2.5 vs MGE 3.0 kpc; 55 % vs 50 % of mass within R<3 kpc), which lifts the learned
v_c peak to **191 km/s** (AGAMA azimuthal), now ~4 % *above* MGE 183 rather than the R=32
model's ~2 % below (179). All within the MGE's own systematic uncertainty.

## Method

New driver `scripts/audit_ngc_r64.py` calls `dgdp.deproject` (default R=64 bundle) on both
galaxies and writes `outputs/real_images/{slug}_arrays.npz` + `{slug}_learned_vs_baseline_
metrics.json` in the format the existing comparison scripts already consume, plus a per-galaxy
summary figure. `outputs/` is gitignored, so this replaces the stale R=32 arrays in place and
the reuse scripts run unchanged:
`plot_ngc4321_figures.py`, `compare_ngc4371_learned_vs_mge.py`, `compare_ngc4371_vc_and_bar.py`,
`plot_ngc4371_density_comparison.py`, and the new `compare_ngc4371_radial_density.py`
(Σ(R) + half-mass radius). Mass conservation is asserted in the driver (learned and baseline
each integrate to M\* to <0.1 %).

**Inputs.** NGC 4321: `NGC4321_m_c_r_f.fits` (WCS), D=15.2 Mpc, i=30°, PA_onsky=153°,
M\*=6×10¹⁰ (README quickstart params). NGC 4371: `NGC4371/final/NGC4371.fits` + `NGC4371_mask.
fits` (WCS-less S4G cutout), 0.75″/pix, D=16.194 Mpc, i=58°, PA_pix=1.8°, centre (254.6,152.8),
M\*=3.53×10¹⁰ (the 2026-06-25 MGE-validation geometry).

## Results — NGC 4371 vs MGE (R=64 vs R=32)

| quantity | R=32 (2026-06-25) | **R=64 (now)** | MGE (Behzad) |
|---|---|---|---|
| RMS\|z\| learned/MGE, R=0.5–8 | 1.16→0.98 | **0.97→1.08** | — |
| RMS\|z\| learned/MGE @ R=12 | 0.80 | **0.98** | — |
| v_c peak, AGAMA azimuthal | 179 | **191** | 183 |
| v_c peak, direct-sum | 175 | **183** | — |
| A2 peak | 0.33 @ 3.4 kpc | **0.33 @ 3.4 kpc** | 0.17 @ 2.3 kpc |
| projected R½ | ~2.7 (image) | **2.5** | 3.0 |
| mass frac R<3 kpc | ~0.51 (image) | **0.55** | 0.50 |

**Interpretation.** Vertical prior: still validated, now tracking the MGE flaring out to
R=12 kpc (the earlier large-R shortfall is gone at higher radial resolution). Bar: the
learned in-plane Σ(R,φ) *is* the deprojected image, so its A2 retains the full m=2 bar the
MGE's few Gaussians smooth away — unchanged by grid resolution. Concentration/v_c: R=64
resolves the central peak better, so the disk is marginally more concentrated than the MGE and
v_c sits just above it; the thick vertical structure still trims v_c by only ~1–2 % vs the
thin baseline. The three effects are consistent (more central mass → higher v_c, smaller R½).

## Caveats / minor items

- **R>12 kpc**: the learned q_m is unconstrained there (edge artifact); RMS|z| and Σ(R)
  beyond R≈12 are not reliable and are shaded/tapered in the figures.
- **x=0 render seam**: the learned edge-on map has a faint vertical seam at x=0 (interpolation
  artifact only; RMS|z| and v_c are unaffected). Enforcing z-symmetry on q_m at the source
  would tidy this — still the clean pipeline TODO noted in the 2026-06-25 report.
- The two `astropy.wcs` SIP-distortion INFO messages on NGC 4321 are harmless (the WCS is
  used only to convert PA_onsky→PA_pixel).
